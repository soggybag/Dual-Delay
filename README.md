# Dual Delay (working name)

Two PT2399 delay lines whose repeats can feed themselves, each other, or a blend of both. Idea stage (2026-10-25).

- **Idea and design plan:** [docs/Ideas.md](docs/Ideas.md)
- **Sibling project:** `../Loop Echo/` (single PT2399 + effects loop in the feedback). The delay block, limiter, LFO and mixer are reused from it (its PT2399 network comes from the user's Small Time, 2022).

- **Schematic:** [docs/DualDelay_schematic.pdf](docs/DualDelay_schematic.pdf), generated by `scripts/build_dualdelay.py` (`DualDelay.kicad_sch`). ERC 0.

**Panel (1590BB):** TIME 1, TIME 2, FB 1, FB 2, TWIST, MIX + SERIES toggle. Internal trims: RATE, DEPTH (LFO on line 2).

**Status:** v0.1 breadboard schematic (2026-10-25). Next: breadboard; check PT2399 and a 16 mm dual-gang B10k pot at JLC.
